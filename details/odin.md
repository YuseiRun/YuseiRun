# Odin  ![Odin](https://img.shields.io/badge/Odin-4A90E2?logo=odin&logoColor=white)

I had followed a [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfgZOEvO0kM) in Odin that loosely recreated Breakout the game. Writing in Odin really felt natural. I cannot remember the video I had watched, but it was a video comparing Zig and Odin. In the video the narrator described Zig as a language trying to be foundational and Odin like a painting and I would agree.
Writing in Odin did feel very natural, almost like talking to the computer if that makes sense. I *was* interested in using Odin on multiple projects.

I say was interested in using Odin, only because I don't believe learning Odin would be very helpful for my career. I need to make myself marketable to more companies than JangaFx. The other problem I had with Odin is the lack of community support, for example I wanted to use Odin to create a simple TUI application for myself (and anyone who wanted to use it), but there was really only one (maybe two) projects that I could use to create the application (one was discontinued and the other a WIP).

## Why did I try Odin?

Honestly, I was really drawn into not only the syntax, but gingerbill's passion for his project. I currently am subscribed to his Youtube channel and rss feed because I do like to hear what he has to say.

## Would I go back to using Odin?

Absolutely! I really enjoyed programming in Odin, it was fun and felt really natural. After the *ONE* project I created, the only two issues I had where the lack of industry utilization and the lack of libraries.

### Sidenote

I really enjoyed gingerbills talk on the importance of math. The presentation he did at the Better Software Conference 2025 plus the breakout tutorial by Karl Zylinski mentioned earlier encouraged me to learn about Linear algebra.
